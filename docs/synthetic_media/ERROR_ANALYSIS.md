## 1. Objective & Methodology

In emergency response contexts, false alarms (False Positives) waste precious dispatch resources, while missed manipulations (False Negatives) expose operational commanders to weaponized disinformation.

This report conducts a forensic failure-mode audit of the predictions generated by:
- **Branch B (CIFAKE Image Forensics):** Holdout test partition ($N=75$: 38 real, 37 synthetic).
- **Branch A (DFD Video Forensics):** Holdout video-level evaluation ($N=2$ test instances).

All observations are derived strictly from measurable properties (file size, resolution, compression artifacts, frequency blur, boundary blending) without subjective fabrication.

### 3.2 Failure Mode Analysis
1. **Severe Cohort Scarcity:** Because the local DFD sample is a controlled development sample ($N=4$ items total), training the temporal head on only 2 reference assets forces conservative weight initialization to avoid explosive saturation.
2. **Actor Biometric Disjointness:** In accordance with strict non-leakage controls, the actors in the test set (`actor_dfd_pair_01`, `actor_dfd_054`) share zero overlap with training actors (`actor_dfd_071`, `actor_dfd_pair_02`). The subtle boundary blending of the face-swap produces output logits near the decision boundary ($\hat{p} \approx 0.417$).
3. **Threshold Sensitivity:** With a threshold $\tau = 0.40$, both samples are accurately identified as manipulated. However, adhering strictly to the pre-specified neutral threshold $\tau = 0.50$, both are classified as False Negatives.

## 4. Operational Mitigations for Emergency Dispatch Pipelines

1. **Dual-Score Flagging:** Downstream crisis prioritization (Phase 6/7) must ingest both `synthetic_probability` ($[0, 1]$) and `quality_status` (`VALID` vs `UNCALIBRATED`).
2. **Soft Continuous Features:** Synthetic media risk must remain a **continuous numerical feature** rather than a hard binary gate. A probability of $0.417$ still elevates the multi-factor risk score relative to a baseline of $0.05$.
3. **Multi-Modal Verification:** Media risk alone must never decide the validity of an incident; it must be correlated with tweet text urgency, burst velocity, and road reachability.
